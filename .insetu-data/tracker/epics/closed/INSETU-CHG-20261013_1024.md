## Description
* **Metronome Job Freeze and Resume**: Introduced `pause_job` and `resume_job` in `akasa.workers` to freeze scheduled jobs (`status='paused'`) without dropping configured intervals or metadata, and restore them with optional timer recalculation.
* **Extension SDK JobManager Expansion**: Added `cancel`, `pause`, and `resume` methods to `JobManager` in `akasa.extension` to expose scheduled task lifecycle controls directly through `ctx.jobs` with tenant workspace scoping.
